MS43 — PMI & Characteristics Integration

Included capabilities:
- NX 2506 PRT and JT viewing with component structure and 3D assembly controls.
- NX-native PMI annotations, dimension graphics, dependencies, and detail tables.
- Companion CHR loading through NX Inspector with PC instances, parameter details,
  and linked PMI visualization when NX exposes the relationship.
- Workspace PRT/CHR and JT companion upload workflows.
- Modern right-panel tabs, collapsed Workspace folders on open, and stable
  Explode/Collapse header control width.

Native file loading requires a configured Siemens NX 2506 installation and
appropriate licenses. Exact available model metadata depends on NX and the file.
